Which one to choose

Each one fits a different job.

Unibox is a good fit if you…

  • need apps that Ninite doesn’t offer
  • want to install, update and uninstall apps in one place
  • want an overview of every app on your PC
  • need a specific version or custom install settings

Ninite is a good fit if you…

  • want to quickly install a few of the most common apps without another program
  • use older Windows 7 or 8
  • are happy to install once and update by running the installer again

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Is Unibox a good alternative to Ninite?

Yes, especially if you want more than a one-off install. Like Ninite, Unibox installs without wizards or bundled extras – but it offers more than 25,000 apps, shows every app on your PC, keeps track of updates and can uninstall apps too.

Will I find the apps I know from Ninite in Unibox?

Common apps from Ninite, such as Chrome, Firefox, VLC, 7-Zip or Notepad++, are in the WinGet repository and therefore in the Unibox catalog. Save them to your own package bundle and next time install them all at once.

Is Unibox free for businesses too?

Yes. Unibox is open source under the MIT license and free to use at home and at work. An MSI installer is available for company-wide deployment.

Why does Unibox need to be installed when Ninite is just a download?

Unibox is a desktop app – that’s the only way it can show what’s installed on your PC, keep track of updates and let installs finish in the background. The installer is 4.3 MB and doesn’t need admin rights.
