Roadmap

Where HubGet is heading next.

Ratings, reviews, sync and publishing work as of version 0.1.14. What’s left is a digitally signed installer – free and open source too.

  1. Digitally signed installer No SmartScreen warning on first launch. The build with free signing by SignPath Foundation and the code signing policy are ready. Waiting for the certificate
  2. Ratings, reviews and screenshots Star ratings, written reviews and current screenshots from users – reviews can be edited, deleted and reported. Live since version 0.1.14
  3. Publishing by developers A wizard turns an installer URL into a WinGet manifest, validates it and helps submit it. Once approved, the app shows up in HubGet. Live since version 0.1.14
  4. Sign-in and sync Sign in with GitHub. Favorites, your package bundle and preferences across your PCs – each area can be turned off and account data deleted at any time. Live since version 0.1.14
