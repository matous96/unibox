Why HubGet

Installing apps should be safe and simple.

Safe – no viruses

Only the official repositories of package managers. Every installer is verified against its SHA-256 checksum before it runs.

Simple

One click instead of setup wizards. No “Next, Next, Finish”, no bundled extras and no ads.

Fully under control

Update and uninstall all your apps in one place – including the ones you installed earlier, some other way.
